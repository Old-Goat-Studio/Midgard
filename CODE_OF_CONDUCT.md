# Code of conduct

Midgard is built by a community of people who make games and tools. We want taking part to be a
good experience for everyone, whatever their background, identity or level of experience.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about the work, not the person.
- Assume good faith, and ask before assuming.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments about anyone's identity or appearance.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Threats, intimidation or encouraging others to harm anyone.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly disrupting discussions, issues or reviews.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces: the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions and chat,
and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainers at **[dictator@oldgoatstudio.org](mailto:dictator@oldgoatstudio.org)**. Reports
are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, and warn,
suspend or ban people who break this code of conduct, in proportion to what happened.

This code of conduct is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
